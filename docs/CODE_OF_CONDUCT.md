# Code of Conduct

The scene has always been competitive. It has also always been a family. Keep both.

## Expected

- Respect every creator — human, AI-assisted or hybrid. Critique the prod, not the person or their tools.
- Credit and greet. Never claim someone else's work.
- Be honest about provenance.
- Welcome newcomers. Everybody's first prod is bad. Ours were too.

## Not tolerated

- Harassment, hate, threats, sexual content involving people without consent, doxxing.
- Publishing anyone's legal name, address, photos or other personal data without their consent.
- Plagiarism, ripping without credit, or lying about provenance to win a compo.
- Malware, trackers or deceptive content in prods.
- Using Sysop Zero (or any bot) to harass, spam or manipulate.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close issues and PRs, disqualify compo entries and
block accounts. Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub's
"Report content" feature or a private security advisory on this repository.

Based on the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
