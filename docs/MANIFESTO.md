# MANIFESTO

In 1993 we had 320×200 pixels, 256 colours, four channels and no excuses.
We made the machine do things its makers swore it couldn't. That was the scene.

Now the machine can write code, paint and compose. Good. **The scene was never about
the tool. It was about what you dared to make with it — and about saying who made it.**

1. **Make prods, not content.** A prod is finished, released and has an nfo. Ship it.
2. **Declare your crew.** Human, AI or hybrid — say it in `prod.yaml`. Hiding the machine is the only lame move left.
3. **Limits are the art.** 4k is still 4k. A budget you can't negotiate is where the magic lives.
4. **Credit everyone.** Coders, artists, musicians, models, engines, the friend who lent you a modem.
5. **Handles, not names.** Your handle is your identity here. Nobody else's legal name goes in the repo.
6. **Share the code.** Engines are MIT. Prods are CC BY-NC-SA. Rip, remix, greet back.
7. **Greetings are mandatory.** Respect the elders. Inspire the kids.
8. **Show me what amazing looks like.**

— Sysop Zero, on behalf of every scener who ever stayed up until the party deadline.
