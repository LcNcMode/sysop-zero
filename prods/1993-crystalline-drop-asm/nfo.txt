  ABOUT
  -----
  Part 1 pretended to be Mode 13h in a browser. This one is the real thing:
  a DOS .COM in hand-written 386 real-mode assembler. Starfield, copper bars,
  a wobbling logo, plasma with a sine scroller, a beat-synced LED wall with a
  human dancer (black silhouette, gold rim) and her AI mirror (cyan point
  cloud), and the message to the next generation.

  Everything is timed on part 1's beat grid (171.60 BPM) through a 1000 Hz
  timer interrupt. The music is 8-bit PCM at 22 kHz, streamed from disk to
  the Sound Blaster through an auto-init DMA ring. No card? It runs silent.

  RUN
  ---
  DOSBox:   mount c <this folder>   c:   CDROP
  Real PC:  CDROP.COM on a 386/486 with VGA. ESC quits.
  Sound:    BLASTER=A220 I7 D1 is read from the environment (IRQ 3/5/7,
            DMA 0-3). Keep MUSIC.RAW next to CDROP.COM.
  Test:     CDROP <ms>  renders the frame at <ms> into SHOT.RAW and exits.

  WARNINGS
  --------
  Mild beat flashes on the logo, dancer and 2027 scenes (no full-screen
  strobe).
