  ABOUT
  -----
  One beam, one tube. Every pixel is drawn by a simulated electron beam on
  an oscilloscope CRT with real phosphor persistence. Each section is a
  "signal" with its own phosphor colour, and the sections morph into each
  other by resampling the beam path. 3:01, synced to 463 kick onsets
  measured offline from the track.

    pre  two real oscillators, panned L/R; the screen is their true XY plot
    00   POWER ON          XANADU PRESENTS, title written stroke by stroke
    01   LISSAJOUS GARDEN  3D Lissajous knots
    02   TRUE XY           a real stereo goniometer of the music

    04   TUNNEL VISION     warp tunnel, words flying at you

    10   LASER CATHEDRAL   two-row haze laser rig, crowd in backlight
    EOT  hand x network, credits, power off

  CONTROLS
  --------
  Click to power on.  F fullscreen  Space pause  Left/Right -/+5 s
  1-9, 0 jump to a signal  C credits  R restart  M reduced flashing

  WARNINGS
  --------
  PHOTOSENSITIVITY: flashes, strobing lasers and fast cuts.
  Press M (or add ?calm to the URL) for a reduced-flashing mode.
