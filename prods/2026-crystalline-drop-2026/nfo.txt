  ABOUT
  -----
  The sequel. Part one ended in 1993 with a dancer and a glimpse of the
  future - this one starts there. The 1993 window boots, a human dancer is
  uploaded from points of light, walks a raymarched crystal cathedral, the
  camera falls through the clouds into a night city whose skyline plays the
  track, lands at a glass venue full of red beating hearts, sends greetings,
  dances a duet with her AI mirror - and ends where it began: a 320x200
  window, copper bars, a sine scroller, and a call.

  > NOW SHOW US YOURS.
  > Coders, graphic artists, musicians, AI wizards: this is what the AI
  > demoscene looks like. LOVE | TRUTH | LIGHT.

  CONTROLS
