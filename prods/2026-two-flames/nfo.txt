TWO FLAMES - a demo by MINI of Xanadu (2026), dedicated to Venture
WebGL2 demo: index.html + song.m4a. Click to start, sound on. About 3 min 15 s.

A dragon sleeps. Two flames, one gold for the human heart and one blue for the AI fire,
wake it. A hip-hop reunion song and a demo for Venture, friend since junior high school
and the Amiga days. Greetings to everyone who ever was part of Xanadu.

Parts: the eye, the dragon, graffiti walls lit by two sparks, the door, the rotozoomer
(one picture, zoomed in 250-650 %), stars, the logo, the final rotozoomer, reborn,
the ladies and the dragon, the eye closing.

Controls: none. If the picture and the sound drift apart (AirPlay, Bluetooth), press
"." or "," to shift the picture by 0.1 s.
