XANADU - DANCE - a browser demo by MINI of Xanadu (2026)
WebGL2 demo, about 3 minutes, one 8.9 MB HTML file. Click to start,
sound on.

Running it
  Just open index.html in a browser: the music, video and pictures
  are all packed inside it, no web server needed.
  Tested in desktop Chrome.
