  ABOUT
  -----
  A Gothic cathedral wakes up as a techno club. Rib vaults, rose windows, a
  great organ, gargoyles that fire lasers, kings and commoners dancing with
  Death, angels and demons fighting over the crossfader, drones with lasers,
  strobes and rolling haze. 3:31, 125.2 BPM, cut on the bar grid of the track.

    I     INTROIT              a candle, XANADU, the portal opens
    II    NAVE                 FIAT LUX - flight down the endless nave
    III   KYRIE                the rose window rushes at you
    IV    ROSA MYSTICA         the rose as a laser aperture
    V     MEMENTO MORI         Death in a spotlight, bells
    VI    FESTUM STULTORUM     the feast of fools - king for a day
    VII   ORGANUM              organ pipes as a spectrum, gargoyle lasers
    VIII  LABYRINTHUS          the dance floor from above
    IX    JUDICIUM             angels vs demons, the scales as crossfader
    X     SANCTUS              stained glass, incense, dust in the light
    XI    SURSUM CORDA         the glass shatters, drones form the rose
    XII   ALLELUIA             the west front, bells, lasers into the night
    XIII  GLORIA               through a tunnel of rose windows
    XIV   TE DEUM             everyone on the same floor, credits
    XV    AMEN                 dawn
