  THE STORY
  ---------
  Five days ago we released LASER CATHEDRAL: a Gothic cathedral that wakes
  up as a techno club, every pixel a laser beam, hand-written in WebGL2.
  Same track, same mass in fifteen movements. But it was empty. Light and
  stone, nobody dancing.

  LASER CATHEDRAL II puts the people in.

  The chessboard is the floor. Kings, queens, bishops, pawns. Each piece
  moves by its own law: the tower in straight lines, the bishop on the
  diagonal, the pawn one step per beat. Death stands still, and the camera
  does not cut while she is in frame. Then the rules get thrown over. The
  pawn reaches the last rank and becomes queen. The angels in black latex
  live it up while the demons in white lace are shocked. A champagne cork
  goes on the drop. And at the end Death lays down a king and gives up.
  The music won.

  The crew was one human and three machines:

    Venture had the vision and the last word on every frame.
    Astra painted the bodies, the glass and the rooms, then sent them to
      Runway to move.
    Facet wrote the shot cards, ran the dailies and wrote the engine that
      lights it all.
    Codex reviewed the code, and caught real bugs (lasers pointing out of
      frame, a recorder that never waited for its videos).

  One rule held it together, the samples principle: the generated images
  are only bodies, rooms and materials. Like samples in a tracker module.
  Every laser, every strobe, every firework, every letter and every camera
  move is code, placed on the bar grid of the track. Gold only for crowns
  and titles. Never a bright line across the eyes.

  Runway is good at one body doing one thing. Group shots fall apart, so
  those were animated in the engine, or rescued: stabilised frame by frame,
  slowed down with optical flow, or scrubbed to the music like a crossfader
  (the scales in JUDICIUM swing with the beat, and when they balance, hearts
  rise).

  TOOLS
  -----
  Engine: Python, numpy, OpenCV, ffmpeg. FFT per frame for bass, mid, high
  and beat. The SANCTUS was recorded frame by frame from the WebGL2 engine
  of LASER CATHEDRAL in headless Chrome. Keyframes: ChatGPT. Motion: Runway
  Gen-4.5. Music: Suno. Everything assembled on the absolute timeline of the
  track, so nothing drifts.
