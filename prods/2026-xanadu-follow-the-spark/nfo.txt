  ABOUT
  -----
  A spark breaks the pattern, crosses a concrete world, and becomes a shared
  moment. Curiosity, new technology and human warmth.
  1280x720, 16:9, 24 fps, about 36.4 seconds. H.264 video, AAC stereo audio.
  Picture, graphics, music and credits are all inside the MP4. Works offline.

  CONTROLS
  --------
  Click "Play with sound". Pause / Restart / Fullscreen under the picture.
  "Production credits" shows the credits. To save the MP4, use the
  download button on the prod's gallery card.
