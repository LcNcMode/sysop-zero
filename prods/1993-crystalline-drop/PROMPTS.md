Chopped looped funk breakbeats, punchy crunchy 8-bit kick and snare, pattern-based arrangement with abrupt pattern switches. Fast tracker arpeggio chords, vibrato and portamento lead melodies, square-wave chip leads, short sampled synth stabs, rave piano, orchestra hit, slap bass and string pad samples looped tight. Euphoric melodic demo-intro and cracktro energy, catchy hooks, sudden key change, breakdown with solo arpeggio then full drop. 125-140 BPM, raw narrow crunchy mix, volume-slide fades. Keep the original melody and chords, re-voiced on tracker instruments. Instrumental.

Exclude: modern EDM, sidechain pumping, wide stereo, polished mastering, trap hi-hats, 808, autotune, live drums, reverb wash
