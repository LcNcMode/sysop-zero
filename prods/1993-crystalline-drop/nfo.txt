  ABOUT
  -----
  Venture is back on the keyboard after more than 30 years. Once in Aros-D,
  then Paramount and finally Xanadu - now teaming up with Claude for one more
  production. Starfield, logo drop, plasma, voxel space, glenz vectors,
  twisters, tunnel, a tribute to The Party 1993 in Herning, fire greetings,
  vector bobs, a 2027 dance scene, bump mapping, metaballs, rotozoom and a
  message to the next generation.

  > This demo is a love letter to 1993. May it inspire young coders, graphic
  > artists, musicians and AI wizards to give the scene a revival. What does
  > the AI demoscene look like in 2027? Show me what amazing looks like!

  CONTROLS
  --------
  tap left / right = skip 10 s   middle = pause
  keys: SPACE, LEFT, RIGHT, ESC
  WARNING: contains strobe and flashing lights (a reduced mode is in the menu)
