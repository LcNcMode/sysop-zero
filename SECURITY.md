# Security

Found a security problem in the repo, the gallery, the automation, or a prod? Thank you for
looking. Please report it **privately**:

1. Open the **Security** tab of this repository.
2. Click **Report a vulnerability** and describe what you found and how to reproduce it.

Please don't open a public issue or PR for security problems, and don't test against other
people's accounts or data.

**In scope:** the gallery site, the workflows and scripts in this repo, and prods that do
something they shouldn't (tracking, malware, escaping the player).

**Out of scope:** GitHub itself, third-party services and archives we link to.

A maintainer will get back to you as soon as possible. Reports made in good faith are welcome,
and we are happy to credit you by handle once a fix is out.

For conduct problems (harassment, personal data, ripping), see the
[Code of Conduct](docs/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
